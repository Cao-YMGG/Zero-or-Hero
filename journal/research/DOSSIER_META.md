# META dossier

Read before every pre-market call and sniper pick. Evidence: `PROFILE_META.md` (2026 minute
data), `underlyings/META.md` (1-year backtest), EVOLUTION.md v6–v10.

## Personality
- Calmer than AMD: intraday vol ~29%, median open→close 1.0%, range 2.6%; only 18 days ≥3%
  and 3 days ≥5% in 2026, but those few are the 25–50x 0DTE days (3/26, 7/09, 9/21).
- **Moves on its own news**: correlation with QQQ/SPY/GOOGL/MSFT/AMZN only 0.37–0.50. Most of
  its biggest days had flat peers (6/01, 6/03, 7/02, 9/01, 9/21, 9/28). The market leads it
  very slightly (5-min corr +0.02–0.03), not enough to trade.
- A fifth of the day's movement is in the first 30 minutes.
- No weekday edge; same-day options Mon/Wed/Fri.

## What has (and hasn't) worked
- Big gaps continue: ≥+2% continued 73% (median +0.8%), ≤−2% continued 64%. Small gaps
  (±1%) mostly fill.
- Early move alone is a coin flip (~50% continuation), except the rare days already ≥3% by
  10:00–10:30 (4 days), which kept going — those were catalyst days.
- Mechanical rules can't find its big days; they come from fresh catalysts with a small gap.
  This stock is for the catalyst sniper, not for mechanical momentum.
- Connect (late September) marked a top; earnings reactions are large both ways (−8.5%
  4/30, −8% 7/30 on capex worries; +10% 1/29).

## Catalyst chain to watch
Product traction (Muse downloads and App Store rank — check daily), AI model launches,
capex guidance (the market punished higher capex in 2026), legal and regulatory rulings
(3/26 verdict −6%), ad-market data, Connect and earnings dates. Meta news also moves the
CPU chain (Muse → INTC/AMD/ARM), so a Meta catalyst is a cue to check AMD.
