# The gap-down put on real option prices, every weekday
Rule: the stock opens below the prior close and is still >= 0.1% under its open at 10:00 -> put at 10:00, nearest expiry (same day on Mon/Wed/Fri, the next listed expiry on Tue/Thu), sold at 3x (`take3`, the live nvda_gapdown_put rule) or 15:45. Costs 10% each way. `ledger` = the contract a $500 ledger buys (one contract <= $150, |delta| >= 0.06), nearest 0.40 delta; `d0.40` = that delta without a budget. Columns: n | win% | avg / without the best / without the best two | median. Data through 2026-11-07. Sunday tests 36 and 46. See zoh/research/gapdown.py.
Qualifying days: 592 (69 with no contract the ledger could buy).
